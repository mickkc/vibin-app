Vibin' is a self-hosted music streaming server that allows you to upload, manage, and stream your music collection from anywhere.

This is the client application, built with Flutter. To set up a self-server, check out <a href="https://vibin.rip/docs/installation" target="_blank">the documentation</a>.

You can also try out the demo instance by connecting to <a href="https://demo.vibin.rip" target="_blank">https://demo.vibin.rip</a> and using the credentials "Demo" for the username, and "DemoDemo" for the password.

Checkout the <a href="https://vibin.rip" target="_blank">website</a> for more information and documentation.

<big>Features:</big>

<ul>
    <li>Connect to your own, self-hosted server.</li>
    <li>Listen to your music without restrictions.</li>
    <li>Invite your friends to join your server.</li>
    <li>Collaborate on playlists with other users.</li>
    <li>Automatically fetch album covers, metadata and lyrics.</li>
    <li>Upload new tracks directly from your device.</li>
    <li>Manage your server from anywhere.</li>
    <li>Bulk edit multiple tracks at once.</li>
    <li>Generate embeddable widgets (iframes, scripts & images).</li>
    <li>Completely open source & private.</li>
</ul>

<b>Vibin' is currently in public beta</b> - I'm working on making it more stable, faster, and easier to set up & use.
